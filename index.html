Research direction

Self-Improving Physical Agents
through Agent–World Model Co-Evolution

I aim to build AI that understands the physical world, acts within it, and grows more capable through experience. My work connects 3D/4D perception, generative world modeling, and memory toward this goal.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Xiaojuan Qi. Previously, I visited Harvard University, working with Yilun Du and Mengyu Wang.

01 / The agent

Learning through interaction.

Self-improving physical agents perceive, reason, and act in the world. I study how purposeful interaction, successes, and failures can become reusable skills and knowledge—so improvement persists across tasks, rather than ending with a single attempt.

02 / The learning loop

Agent and world model, together.

I investigate a reciprocal loop: the agent gathers informative experience to refine an action-conditioned 4D world model; the model helps compare possible futures and guide further exploration. Real outcomes update both the model and the agent’s strategies and memory.

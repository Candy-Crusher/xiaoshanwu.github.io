Xiaoshan Wu 吴小山

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Hong Kong, advised by Xiaojuan Qi, and a Visiting Scholar at Harvard University, working with Yilun Du and Mengyu Wang.

I am interested in world models for generalizable robotic planning. My work connects spatial perception and memory with physically grounded future prediction to support reliable robot action.

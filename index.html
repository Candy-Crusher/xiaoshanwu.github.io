Xiaoshan Wu 吴小山

I am a Visiting Scholar at Harvard University, working with Yilun Du and Mengyu Wang on 4D world models for robotic planning. I am in the fourth year of my Ph.D. at the University of Hong Kong, under the supervision of Xiaojuan Qi. I have been awarded the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship (HKPFS) and the HKU Presidential Scholarship (HKUPS). Previously, I received dual B.S. degrees in Computer Engineering from Zhejiang University and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

I am interested in world models for generalizable robotic planning: how predictions of the physical world can support reliable action in unfamiliar environments. My work develops the perceptual and predictive foundations for this goal, from recovering and retaining spatial evidence to imagining physically plausible 4D futures that guide robot actions and support pre-execution consistency checks. I am now interested in agent–world model co-evolution: using task-relevant prediction errors to guide interaction and model refinement, while action-conditioned world models support policy evaluation, improvement, and longer-horizon planning.

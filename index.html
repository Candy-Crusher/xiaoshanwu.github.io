Xiaoshan Wu

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at The University of Hong Kong, supervised by Dr. Xiaojuan Qi and Dr. Zhongrui Wang. I received my B.Eng. degree from Zhejiang University and the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.

My research focuses on high-resolution, temporally-consistent 3D/4D scene reconstruction and perception, particularly under challenging conditions such as dynamic motion, low light, or extreme illumination. I explore fusion of event-based and frame-based inputs, unified token representation, and video-spatial VLMs.

World models, memory, and embodied intelligence

I study how robots can use world models to reason about actions and improve through interaction. My work connects 4D world–action models, video generation, and geometry-grounded memory for spatial reasoning. My longer-term goal is to build agents that test alternatives in learned environments and turn real-world feedback into reusable knowledge.

Earlier work on event-based perception and neuromorphic computing informs my approach to reliable perception and efficient learning in the physical world.

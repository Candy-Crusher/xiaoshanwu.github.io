Research vision: Self-Improving Physical Agents through Agent–World Model Co-Evolution.

I study physical agents that turn interaction into cumulative improvement. My work focuses on action-conditioned 3D/4D world models, embodied spatial reasoning, and closed-loop robot learning for robust planning and control.

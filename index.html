Xiaoshan Wu 吴小山

Ph.D. candidate · The University of Hong Kong

World models and memory
for embodied intelligence.

I connect 3D/4D perception, video generation, and spatial reasoning to help robots understand and act in the physical world. My longer-term goal is to build agents that improve through interaction and feedback.

At HKU, I am advised by Prof. Xiaojuan Qi. Previously, I visited Harvard University, working with Yilun Du and Mengyu Wang.

Verifiable 4D World–Action Model for Robust Robot Control

X. Wu*, R. Cai*, Y. Bao, et al.

Under review at ICLR 2027Co-first author

S4NDBOX predicts appearance, geometry, and motion before generating actions. Agreement between the predicted future and its paired action provides a geometric signal for candidate selection before execution.

+13.3 percentage points on RoboTwin Hard over RGB-only after clean-only task training; zero-shot sim-to-real on a Franka Research 3.

GaMe: Geometry as Memory for Multi-View Spatial Reasoning

X. Wu, X. Lyu, Y. Yu, et al.

Under review at ICRA 2027First author

A 16-slot geometric working memory combines evidence across views and augments the original visual–language context for spatial question answering.

VideoSSM: Autoregressive Long Video Generation with Hybrid State-Space Memory

Y. Yu*, X. Wu*, X. Hu, et al.

arXiv preprint, 2025Co-first author

Local attention preserves recent detail while recurrent state-space memory retains long-range context, supporting autoregressive long-video generation without an ever-growing KV cache.

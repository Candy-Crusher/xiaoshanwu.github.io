About

A coherent understanding of the world, in service of meaningful action.

I’m Xiaoshan Wu （吴小山）.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Xiaojuan Qi. Previously, I visited Harvard University, working with Yilun Du and Mengyu Wang.

I received my dual bachelor’s degrees in Computer Engineering from Zhejiang University and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign through the ZJUI Institute.

Research perspective

I study spatial intelligence for physical agents: how to build a coherent understanding of the physical world that supports reliable action in unfamiliar environments.

My work develops the perceptual and predictive foundations for this goal, from recovering and retaining spatial evidence to imagining physically plausible futures that guide actions and support pre-execution consistency checks.

Looking ahead

I am now interested in extending these foundations toward agent–world model co-evolution, where agents gather informative experience to improve world models, while improved models support long-horizon planning and action evaluation.

A personal inspiration

Joi in Blade Runner 2049 inspires my interest in AI as a presence in everyday life. For me, the research question is how to ground that presence in an understanding of the world, meaningful action, and the ability to learn from experience.

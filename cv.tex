% ==================== RESEARCH INTERESTS ====================
\cvsection{Research Interests}
\textbf{Self-Improving Physical Agents through Interactive 4D World Models.}
I study how robots can turn purposeful interaction into cumulative, transferable capability. My research combines robust perception, action-responsive 4D foundation world models, and persistent world and experience memory, enabling agents to explore informative interventions in learned surrogate environments, revise strategies from feedback, and transfer the resulting improvements to the physical world under limited interaction budgets.

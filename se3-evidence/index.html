02 / EVALUATION PROTOCOL

What the tracker sees.
What it never sees.

We keep the source of supervision separate from the source of predictions. The benchmark tests relative object motion, not zero-shot canonical absolute pose.

01

Observation

RGB · metric depth · camera intrinsics · first-frame object mask.

→
02

Rigid tracking

SAM2 + TAPIR + Point2Pose rigid registration; predict future 4×4 SE(3).

→
03

Isolated GT scoring

Only after inference, align with GT pose at frame 0 and measure future translation/rotation error.

✓

What this supports

Precise relative SE(3) tracking for one real rigid-object interaction sequence under RGB-D observation and object-mask initialization.

!

What it does not yet support

Generalization to unseen objects, long occlusion, arbitrary camera motion, end-to-end automatic object discovery, or calibrated rejection. Tracking “not lost” is not a confidence certificate.

04 / RESEARCH DECISION

Promising as a
rigid-label candidate.

The first real-GT pilot clears the proposed accuracy threshold comfortably. The next question is not whether this clip looks good — it is whether the method keeps working across unseen objects, occlusions, motion regimes and camera setups.

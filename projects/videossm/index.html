VideoSSM: Autoregressive Long Video Generation with Hybrid State-Space Memory

Yifei Yu*, Xiaoshan Wu*, Xinting Hu, Tao Hu, Yangtian Sun, Xiaoyang Lyu, Bo Wang, Lin Ma, Yuewen Ma, Zhongrui Wang, Xiaojuan Qi
The University of Hong Kong · PICO, ByteDance · Southern University of Science and Technology

PaperPDFBibTeX

TL;DRA local attention cache preserves recent detail while recurrent state-space memory carries evolving context beyond the window.

Overview

VideoSSM augments autoregressive diffusion with a hybrid memory: a sliding-window cache for local detail and an evolving state for longer-range context. It supports minute-long generation and prompt switching without retaining an ever-growing history of keys and values.

Method

Remember the past. Keep the scene moving.

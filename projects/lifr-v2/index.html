LiFR v2: Completion-Augmented Event Propagation for High-Rate Dense Prediction

Tao Wan*, Xiaoshan Wu*†, Yifei Yu, Bo Wang, Xiaoyang Lyu, Muxin Liu, Aoxuan Pan, Zhongrui Wang, Xiaojuan Qi
Southern University of Science and Technology · The University of Hong Kong
* Equal contribution · † Project lead

Under review · IEEE TPAMI

PaperPDFBibTeXRepository

TL;DRPropagation reuses observed content, event-guided completion recovers missing source support, and memory retains inferred content for later queries.

Overview

LiFR v2 supports causal anytime and streaming segmentation, depth and joint prediction. An Event-Guided Completion Module addresses regions missing from the RGB keyframe; a History Retrieval Module reuses completed features. SHF-Emerge tests rapid emergence and disocclusion.

Method

Beyond propagation alone.

01

Propagation

02

Completion

Recover event-supported content absent from the keyframe.

Retention
